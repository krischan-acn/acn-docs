# Design voting (A/B)

A hidden tool built into the newsroom that lets ACN staff compare two versions of a design and vote for the one they prefer. Readers never see it.

::: info Built, but nothing to vote on yet
The tool works, but no comparisons have been set up. A developer adds them; see [the newsroom build notes](/build/newsroom#design-voting-a-b).
:::

## Opening it

On any newsroom page, press **F2**, or click the small grey dot in the top bar. Sign in with the employee login your manager gives you. It's never written on this site.

## Comparing

When a page has a comparison, the bar at the bottom offers four views:

- **A**: the page as it is now.
- **B**: the proposed change.
- **Side by side**: both at once.
- **Slider**: drag across to wipe between them.

Then vote **Prefer A** or **Prefer B**. You get one vote per comparison, and you can change it. You don't see other people's votes; that keeps the vote fair.

## Results

People with admin rights see the tallies at `/admin` on the newsroom, and can open or close voting there. Everyone else gets "not found" at that address, which is on purpose.
