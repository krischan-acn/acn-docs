## Opening the admin

Ask a teammate for the admin's address and bookmark it. It is deliberately not written on this public site. The three sections are in the top-left corner: **Companies · Press Releases · Events**.

## The one thing to remember

**Your edits are saved in your own browser until you commit them.**
- Nobody else sees them until then.
- If you clear your browser data, or switch to a different computer, uncommitted edits are not there.

[How saving works](/guide/admin) explains this properly.
